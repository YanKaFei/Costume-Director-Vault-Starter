# 静态生图提示词模板（copy-ready）

> 与 `scripts/recommend.py` 的 `prompt_image` 字段同构；可直接复制进 Midjourney / SDXL / DALL·E / 即梦等工具。
> 原则：先服装锁定，后环境/光线，最后美术与工具参数；不许把风格词替代具体单品。

## 1. 结构公式

```text
[人物与景别] + [服装逐层描述：upper / lower or one-piece / outer / footwear / accessory]
+ [颜色与材质] + [结构细节：领型/门襟/褶裥/闭合]
+ [场景：地点/时间/天气] + [光线] + [美术方向] + [严格度措辞]
+ [连续性锁定] + [画质/镜头语言] + [工具参数]
```

## 2. 逐层描述（每层一行，便于替换）

```text
SUBJECT: full-body character design of an adult <角色>, <职业/身份>, <年龄段>, <体态>
UPPER: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en>
LOWER: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en>
ONE-PIECE: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en>
OUTER: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en>
FOOTWEAR: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en>
ACCESSORY: <GAR-ID> <prompt_en> (position: <左肩/右腕/腰间>)
PARTS: <STR-ID> <结构说明>
MATERIALS: <MAT-ID> <手感/光泽/垂坠>
PALETTE: <2–4 色，明确主色/辅色/点缀色>
SETTING: <地点>, <时间>, <天气>, <季节>
LIGHTING: <key light 方向/色温/反差/实用光源>
ART DIRECTION: <STY-ID 或风格词，不要替代单品>
FIDELITY: <historical | inspired | fictional 对应措辞>
CONTINUITY: <item IDs + colors + accessory positions + wetness/damage state>
QUALITY: photorealistic fabric texture, visible seams and garment construction, cinematic costume reference
TOOL: midjourney → --ar 2:3 --style raw --v 6；sdxl → highly detailed costume sheet；dalle → 自然语言即可
```

## 3. 负向约束（复制）

```text
--no costume morphing, unexpected accessories, color shift, logo, text, watermark,
extra layers, warped hands, deformed body, changing hairstyle, modern zippers (historical)
```

## 4. 填好的两个示例

### 4.1 现代雨夜调查者（fictional，低预算）

```text
full-body character design of an adult urban investigator in a fictional modern city,
upper body: off-white cotton stand-collar shirt, straight fit, slightly wrinkled fabric;
outer: charcoal knee-length wool overcoat, single-breasted, wet hem;
lower: dark charcoal straight wool trousers, pressed crease;
footwear: worn black leather lace-up boots, rubber sole;
accessory: aged brown canvas shoulder bag on left shoulder, worn edges;
palette: charcoal, off white, black;
setting: empty city street, night, heavy rain; lighting: cool moonlight with warm sodium practicals;
art direction: restrained film noir styling, low saturation, hard key light;
original fictional-world costume design, explicitly not a historical reconstruction;
realistic fabric texture, visible seams and construction details, cinematic costume reference;
continuity lock: GAR-EXAMPLE-001 off-white cotton shirt | charcoal wool overcoat | dark trousers |
worn leather boots | left-shoulder canvas bag; wetness=dry; damage=none;
keep all IDs, colors, materials and accessory positions constant across shots
--ar 2:3 --style raw --v 6
```

### 4.2 1930 年代上海知识分子（historical：必须挂真实 GAR/STR/MAT/SRC ID）

```text
full-body character design of an adult Chinese intellectual in 1930s Shanghai,
upper body: <GAR-ID> off-white cotton stand-collar shirt, straight fit;
lower: <GAR-ID> charcoal straight wool trousers;
outer: <GAR-ID> charcoal knee-length wool overcoat, single-breasted;
footwear: <GAR-ID> worn black leather lace-up boots;
structure: <STR-ID> stand collar, one-button closure; <STR-ID> single-breasted placket;
materials: <MAT-ID> worsted wool matte structured drape; <MAT-ID> plain-weave cotton soft wrinkles;
palette: charcoal, off white, black;
setting: rain-wet Shanghai street, night, early winter; lighting: cool key light with warm shop-window practicals;
art direction: restrained urban period styling;
historically grounded period costume, period-accurate construction and materials, no modern fashion elements;
realistic fabric texture, visible seams and construction details, cinematic costume reference;
continuity lock: <IDs>; wetness=dry; damage=none
--ar 2:3 --style raw --v 6
```

> 历史模式必须用 `04-来源/` 中真实 SRC 卡支撑，并在正文列出 SRC ID + URL；不得把模型记忆当来源。

## 5. 一致性检查（复制为交付前 checklist）

- [ ] 每个服装层都有真实卡片 ID（或明确 gap），无编造 ID
- [ ] 颜色数量 ≤ 4，主色/辅色明确
- [ ] 材质与天气/动作匹配（雨：羊毛/皮革/华达呢；舞：弹性/垂坠）
- [ ] 严格度措辞与 fidelity_mode 一致
- [ ] 连续性锁定字段完整（ID、颜色、材质、配饰位置、状态）
- [ ] 负向约束覆盖变形、突变、额外配饰、颜色漂移
- [ ] historical 模式有真实来源；无来源处标 uncertainty
