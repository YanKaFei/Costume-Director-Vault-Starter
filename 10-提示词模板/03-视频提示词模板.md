# 视频提示词模板（copy-ready）

> 与 `scripts/recommend.py` 的 `prompt_video` 字段同构；面向 Sora / Runway / Kling / 即梦等视频生成工具。
> 关键：镜头运动 + 动作 + 布料动态 + 服装锁定 + 状态切换规则。视频最易发生服装漂移，锁定字段必须逐镜重复。

## 1. 结构公式

```text
[镜头运动与景别] + [人物与动作] + [场景/时间/天气]
+ [服装逐层锁定：item IDs + 颜色 + 材质 + 结构]
+ [布料动态：走路/跑动/舞蹈/风雨/坐下]
+ [光线与色彩] + [严格度措辞]
+ [连续性锁定 + 状态切换] + [负向约束]
```

## 2. 分镜表（复制为项目文档）

| 镜头 | 景别/运动 | 时间 | 天气 | 动作 | 服装状态 | 锁定属性 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 中景跟拍 | Day 2 夜 | 雨前干 | 快走 | wetness=dry, damage=none | 大衣至膝/三粒扣/左肩包 |
| S2 | 近景手持 | Day 2 夜 | 大雨 | 跑入雨棚 | wetness=wet（显式切换） | 同上 + 湿痕位置 |
| S3 | 中景固定 | Day 2 夜 | 室内 | 翻找桌面 | wetness=drying, 包仍在左肩 | 同上 |
| S4 | 全景 | Day 3 日 | 晴 | 走出大楼 | wetness=dry（显式切换）, 新增雨伞 | 大衣同款；伞为新增 prop |

> 同一 Look 的固定属性（ID、颜色、材质、配饰位置）在所有分镜必须逐字一致；只有状态（wetness/damage/dirt/新增道具）可以按上表显式切换。

## 3. 单镜头视频 prompt 模板

```text
<镜头运动: slow tracking shot / handheld follow / static medium shot>, <景别>,
<角色> <动作> in <场景>, <时间>, <天气>;
costume lock: <逐层：slot: <GAR-ID> <name_en> [<color>; <material>; <part IDs>]>;
fabric motion: <布料动态：大衣下摆随步伐摆动/湿羊毛变重下坠/裙摆飞扬>;
lighting: <光线>; art direction: <美术>;
<fidelity 措辞>;
continuity: same item IDs, colors, materials and accessory positions as previous shot;
state: wetness=<dry|wet|drying>, damage=<none|...>, dirt=<none|...>;
negative constraints: no costume morphing, no unexpected accessories, no color shift,
no extra layers, no changing hairstyle, no warped hands, no logo/text/watermark.
```

## 4. 已填写示例（虚构示例，非史实）

```text
slow handheld follow shot, medium shot, Lin walking quickly down a rain-slicked street at night in heavy rain;
costume lock: upper body: GAR-EXAMPLE-001 off-white cotton stand-collar shirt [off_white; cotton];
outer: charcoal knee-length wool overcoat [charcoal; wool, gabardine];
lower: dark charcoal straight wool trousers [charcoal; wool];
footwear: worn black leather lace-up boots [black; leather, rubber];
accessory: aged brown canvas shoulder bag on left shoulder [brown; canvas];
fabric motion: heavy wool coat hem swings with each stride, wet wool darkens and hangs heavier;
off-white cotton shirt collar stays closed under the coat;
lighting: cool moonlight rim light with warm sodium practicals; art direction: restrained noir;
original fictional-world costume design, explicitly not a historical reconstruction;
continuity: same item IDs, colors, materials and accessory positions as the previous shot;
state: wetness=wet, damage=none, dirt=none; no instant drying between shots;
negative constraints: no costume morphing, no unexpected accessories, no color shift,
no extra layers, no changing hairstyle, no warped hands, no logo, text or watermark.
```

## 4b. 逐场 prompt 隔离（P3）

- 顶层 prompt 只是**换装参考表**，不得把多场动作/天气拼成一句。
- 逐场数据在 `continuity.scenes[].prompt_image/prompt_video`；每场只写本场 action/weather，
  并带 `wetness=/damage=/dirt=` 状态与同一段 continuity lock。
- 卡片 prompt_en 中的上下文串文（如 "worn in snow" 出现在室内戏、未选中的
  "white t-shirt and leather jacket"）必须剔除；只保留已选 items 的描述。

## 5. 多场次连续性案例模板

```text
CHARACTER: <角色>   LOOK: <LOOK-ID 或 assembled A>   FIDELITY: <historical|inspired|fictional>
LOCKED ITEMS: <GAR/STR/MAT ID + name_en + color + material + accessory position>
STATE TABLE:
  S1 story_day=1 weather=dry   wetness=dry   damage=none   dirt=none
  S2 story_day=1 weather=rain  wetness=wet   damage=none   dirt=none   (transition: S1→S2)
  S3 story_day=2 weather=indoor wetness=drying damage=none  dirt=none   (transition: S2→S3)
  S4 story_day=3 weather=dry   wetness=dry   damage=minor tear, left cuff   (explicit wardrobe note)
VERDICT CRITERIA:
  - item IDs identical across S1–S4
  - colors/materials/accessory positions identical
  - wetness/damage change only at listed transitions
  - prompts carry the same continuity lock string in every shot
```

## 6. 负向约束（视频专用）

```text
no costume morphing between shots, no unexpected accessories,
no color shift, no extra layers appearing or disappearing,
no changing hairstyle between shots, no warped hands, no deformed body,
no logo, text or watermark, no instant drying between shots,
no modern zippers / sneakers (historical mode), no unstable footwear (dance/action)
```

## 7. 交付前检查

- [ ] 每一镜都重复同一段 continuity lock（不能只在第一镜写）
- [ ] 状态变化在分镜表里有明确 transition 行
- [ ] 布料动态与材质一致（湿羊毛更重、丝绸反光、棉麻起皱）
- [ ] 动作与服装安全约束一致（舞蹈不用高跟鞋；追逐不穿限制步幅的长裙）
- [ ] 严格度措辞与 fidelity_mode 一致；historical 卡有真实 SRC 来源
