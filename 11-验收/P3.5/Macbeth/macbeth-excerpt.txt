ACT I

SCENE III. A heath.

[Thunder. Enter the three Witches.]

First Witch: Where hast thou been, sister?
Second Witch: Killing swine.
Third Witch: Sister, where thou?

[Enter Macbeth and Banquo.]

MACBETH: So foul and fair a day I have not seen.
BANQUO: How far is ’t call’d to Forres?

First Witch: All hail, Macbeth! hail to thee, thane of Glamis!
Second Witch: All hail, Macbeth! hail to thee, thane of Cawdor!
Third Witch: All hail, Macbeth! that shalt be king hereafter.

MACBETH: why do you dress me in borrowed robes?

ACT I

SCENE V. Inverness. A room in Macbeth’s castle.

[Enter Lady Macbeth, reading a letter.]

LADY MACBETH: Glamis thou art, and Cawdor; and shalt be
What thou art promised.
Come, thick night,
And pall thee in the dunnest smoke of hell.

ACT II

SCENE II. The same.

[Enter Lady Macbeth.]

LADY MACBETH: That which hath made them drunk hath made me bold.
[Enter Macbeth.]
MACBETH: Macbeth does murder sleep.
LADY MACBETH: Out, damned spot! out, I say!

ACT V

SCENE I. Dunsinane. A room in the castle.

[Enter a Doctor of Physic and a Waiting-Gentlewoman.]

LADY MACBETH: Out, damned spot! out, I say!—One; two.
All the perfumes of Arabia will not sweeten this little hand.
