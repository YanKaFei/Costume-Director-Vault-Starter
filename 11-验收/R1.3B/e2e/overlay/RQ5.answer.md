## Card

- **ID:** STR-0012
- **Name:** 翻领与驳领 / **turndown collar and lapel** (`garment_part`, evidence_status: **verified**, source: **SRC-0118**)
- **Description:** The neckline/front-opening structure of coats and shirts. The **turndown collar** is a collar stand + collar face that folds over along the neckline, with pointed, rounded, or square tips; the **lapel** is the upper section of the jacket's front edge folded outward. They often appear together but are structurally distinct.
