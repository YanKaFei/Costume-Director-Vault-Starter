## 可支撑事实（verified）

- AAT 首选词 coats (garments)；原页 Note："Main garments usually fitted to the upper body, extending below the hip line, open at the front or side and generally having sleeves. Also, similar outer garments worn for warmth or protection from the weather."
- 定义同时覆盖「合身上衣式外套」与「御寒/防风雨外衣」两种用法，可用于区分 coat 与 jacket、cloak。
- 可用于把 trench coat、overcoat、topcoat 等子类挂到同一受控术语下。
