# AAT 词条：scarves（围巾/披巾）

**来源**：Art & Architecture Thesaurus Full Record Display (Getty Research)

## 可支撑事实（verified）

- AAT 首选词 scarves (costume accessories)；原页 Note："Pieces of cloth made in varying widths and lengths and worn for decoration or warmth across the shoulders, around the neck, over the head, or about the waist."
- 定义明确其多功能性：肩、颈、头、腰均可，动机为装饰或保暖。
- 可作为颈/肩/头/腰多部位配饰的术语入口，并与 shawl、veil 区分。
