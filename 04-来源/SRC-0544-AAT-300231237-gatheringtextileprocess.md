## 可支撑事实（verified 来源）

- AAT 首选词 gathering (textile process)；原文 Note："Bunching folds of textile together by pulling on a thread stitched through it for this purpose or on one or more of its constituent filaments, to create fullness."
- 可支撑「抽褶」定义：抽紧缝线使织物聚拢起褶、制造丰满量。
