## 可支撑事实（verified 来源）

- AAT 首选词 pockets (costume components)；原文 Note："Flat pouchlike components stitched into or onto a garment, accessible through a finished opening typically convenient to the hand."
- 可支撑「口袋结构」定义：缝合于衣身内/外的扁平袋状部件，通过完成开口取物；可涵盖 patch/welt/flap 的袋体层面。
