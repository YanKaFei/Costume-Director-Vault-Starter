## 可支撑事实（verified）

- AAT 首选词为 dresses (garments)；原页 Note 定义："Main garments for women, children, or infants consisting of a bodice and skirt made in one or more pieces."
- 层级归属：main garments → clothing → ... → Furnishings and Equipment (hierarchy name)。
- 可用于把「连衣裙 / 袍服 / 礼服」等中文口语词统一映射到受控术语，避免与 skirt、gown 混用。
