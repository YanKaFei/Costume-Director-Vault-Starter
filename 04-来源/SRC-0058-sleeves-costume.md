# AAT 词条：sleeves（袖子/袖型）

**来源**：Art & Architecture Thesaurus Full Record Display (Getty Research)

**发布者**：Getty Research Institute — Art & Architecture Thesaurus (AAT)

**来源类型**：vocabulary

## 可支撑事实（verified）

- AAT 首选词 sleeves (costume)；原页 Note："Shaped coverings for the arm. Originally made separate from the main garment and attached by lacing through eyelets at the shoulder; later, often made as component parts of garments."
- 定义记录重要结构史：袖最初可独立制作，通过肩部孔眼系带连接；后来多作为衣身部件。
- 可作为结构类（袖型/袖窿/可拆卸袖）卡片的一手术语依据。
