## 2. Checks performed

- term accuracy (`name_en`/`part_family`, card ID, CJK/wikilink/typo checks);
- structure location (`STRUCTURE` block, part family);
- material evidence (card materials vs prompt; unknown material assertion = ERROR);
- view consistency (full-body/macro/detail/flat; multiple classes flagged);
- conflicting attributes (matte/glossy, fitted/oversized, front/back, sleeve types,
  waist types);
- cross-card homogeneity (content tokens after removing shared template vocabulary);
- semantic consistency with card `prompt_en` (token sequence ratio).

Unknown physical properties are not guessed; prompts that assert material without
card evidence are errors. Statuses: TEXT_VALID / TEXT_INVALID; the last three
levels are explicitly NOT_RUN pending human/domain/image work.
